# 📌 Project Overview

Retail companies generate massive amounts of transactional data every day. Turning this data into meaningful business insights is essential for improving customer experience, increasing sales, and optimizing marketing strategies.

This project analyzes **3,900 customer purchase records** to understand customer behaviour, purchasing patterns, revenue generation, customer segmentation, subscription impact, and product performance.

The project follows a complete Business Intelligence workflow:

> **Python → PostgreSQL → SQL → Power BI**

# 💡 Key Insights

- Young Adult customers generated the highest revenue.
- Clothing category contributed the highest sales.
- Express shipping customers spent more per transaction.
- Loyal customers formed the largest customer segment.
- Discount campaigns significantly influenced purchasing behaviour.
- Subscription status impacted customer retention and revenue.

---

# 📢 Business Recommendations

- Introduce personalized loyalty programs.
- Increase subscriber-exclusive benefits.
- Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
- Promote top-rated products through targeted advertising.
- Optimize discount strategies to improve profit margins.
